How to Join Retail DAO

Welcome to Retail DAO! This guide outlines the steps to join our community, participate in our Discord server, and gain access to exclusive token-gated channels and activities. Whether you’re new to the DAO or looking to deepen your involvement, here’s how to get started.

Step 1: Join the Discord Server​

  • Access: Joining the Retail DAO Discord server is free and open to everyone.
  • How to Join:
    1. Visit our Discord server.
    2. Follow the onboarding instructions in the welcome channel to verify your account and agree to the community rules.
  • What You Get: Access to public channels for general discussions, announcements, and community engagement.
  • Note: Some channels, such as governance forums, alpha chats, and weekly calls, are token-gated and require holding $RETAIL tokens.

Step 2: Acquire $RETAIL Tokens​

To unlock token-gated channels and participate fully in Retail DAO (e.g., governance, weekly calls, and high-value discussions), you need to hold $RETAIL tokens. Here are the ways to acquire them:

Option 1: Earn Tokens via QuestN and Engage Bot Tasks​

  • QuestN Campaigns:
    • Participate in campaigns like the 2-for-1 Airdrop on QuestN.
    • Complete tasks such as writing X threads or analyzing Pre-TGE projects to earn $RETAIL tokens (e.g., 60,000 $RETAIL for eligible participants).
    • Ensure all tasks are verified and meet quality requirements, as outlined in the Airdrop Instructions.
  • Engage Bot Tasks:
    • Complete tasks assigned by the Engage bot on Discord, such as contributing to discussions, sharing insights, or participating in community events.
    • Tasks vary in complexity and reward, so check the bot’s instructions in the relevant Discord channels.
  • Note: Tokens earned through QuestN or Engage bot tasks are distributed to your connected wallet, typically after a campaign ends.

Option 2: Contribute Meaningfully to Retail DAO​

  • Make valuable contributions to the DAO, such as:
    • Submitting high-quality governance proposals (see Proposal Guidelines).
    • Sharing alpha insights in the Alpha Zone channels (see Discord Rules).
    • Participating in community initiatives or helping with DAO projects.
  • Contributions are reviewed by the community or moderators, and rewards in $RETAIL tokens may be granted based on impact and quality.
  • Check the Governance and Voting Mechanisms pages for ways to get involved.

Option 3: Purchase $RETAIL Tokens on Uniswap​

  • How to Buy:
    1. Visit Uniswap and connect your wallet (e.g., MetaMask).
    2. Select the $RETAIL token pair from our liquidity pools.
    3. Swap a supported cryptocurrency (e.g., ETH) for $RETAIL tokens.
  • Note: Ensure you’re using the official $RETAIL token contract address, available in our Tokenomics documentation, to avoid scams.

Step 3: Unlock Token-Gated Channels​

  • Token-Gated Access:
    • Holding $RETAIL tokens grants access to exclusive Discord channels, including:
      • Governance forums for submitting and voting on proposals.
      • Alpha Zone channels for high-value crypto discussions.
      • Weekly community calls for updates and networking.
    • The Engage bot verifies your token balance to unlock these channels.
  • How to Set Up:
    1. Connect your wallet to the Engage bot in Discord (follow instructions in the token-gated channels).
    2. Ensure your wallet holds $RETAIL tokens.
    3. The bot will automatically grant access to token-gated channels based on your balance.
  • Note: Token requirements for specific channels may vary. Check the Discord Rules for details.

Step 4: Engage with the Community on X​

  • Retail DAO actively engages with the crypto community on X for networking, alpha sharing, and updates.
  • How to Participate:
    1. Follow the official Retail DAO account: @retaildao.
    2. Share your insights, join discussions, or post threads about crypto projects, tagging @retaildao when relevant.
    3. Participate in X-based campaigns (e.g., the 2-for-1 airdrop thread task) to earn rewards and connect with Crypto Twitter.
  • Benefits: Engaging on X boosts your visibility in the crypto community and may qualify you for additional rewards or airdrops.

Step 5: Attend Weekly Calls​

  • Access: Weekly community calls are exclusive to $RETAIL token holders.
  • Purpose: Discuss DAO updates, governance proposals, and networking opportunities.
  • How to Join:
    • Ensure you have access to token-gated channels via the Engage bot.
    • Check the Discord events or announcements channel for call schedules and links.
  • Note: Calls are a great way to stay informed and contribute ideas directly to the DAO.

Get Started Today!​

Join Retail DAO to connect with a vibrant crypto community, contribute to governance, and access exclusive insights. Start by joining our Discord server, earning or purchasing $RETAIL tokens, and engaging on X. For more details, explore our documentation:

  • Discord Rules
  • Proposal Guidelines
  • Voting Mechanisms
  • Airdrop Instructions
  • Tokenomics

Happy contributing, and welcome to Retail DAO! 🚀
