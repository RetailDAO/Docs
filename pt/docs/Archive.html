Proposal Archive

This document contains a list of old governance proposals. This list is going to be updated based on RetailDAO evolution over time.

Jun 25, 2025 - CryptoEQ Premium Token Gated Access RetailDAO Partnership - DAO Treasury (Aragon): Link​

A strategic partnership between CryptoEQ and Retail DAO. The collaboration aims to offer subsidized CryptoEQ Premium memberships to Retail DAO members through an innovative token-gated model. This initiative is designed to enhance the utility of the $RETAIL token, foster a more engaged and informed community, and incentivize token retention. For CryptoEQ, the partnership seeks to increase brand visibility and drive top-line growth.

May 24, 2025 - Budget Proposal Period May-Aug 2025 - DAO Treasury (Aragon): Link​

Periodic budget proposal by the contributors team for token allocations for the next period until August 2025. This proposal consists of 3 parts:

  1. budget request from Treasury (including 2 transactions from Treasury),
  2. claim of vested tokens from vesting contract (1 transaction to Treasury),
  3. 1 governance amendment for proposal thresholds that may be funded by the Main Multisig Wallet.

May 20, 2025 - Tweaking the Retail DAO Membership Tiers - Snapshot: Link​

The proposal adjusts the membership tiers from 20k (Basic), 40k (Pro), and 100k (VIP) to:

  • 20k (Basic),
  • 100k (Pro), and
  • 250k (VIP) to better align with the perceived value of higher tiers in the context of current and future market position of $RETAIL.

May 20, 2025 - Business Development and Partnership Initiatives at Dubai Crypto Week - Snapshot: Link​

Proposal for a business development initiative at Dubai Crypto Week 2025 to secure partnerships, boost community growth, and enhance brand visibility through networking, content creation, and strategic engagements, funded by a 1,000,000 $RETAIL grant.

Apr 14, 2025 - Change in Main Multisig Wallet Signers and Policy Change - Snapshot: Link​

The following changes will be requested for the Main Multisig Wallet plus a policy change for future wallet signers changes:

  • Add 0x6C61...5173 to the multisig wallet as a signer
  • Remove wallet 0x44C6...0934 from the signers
  • Reduce the requirement for Multisig Wallet signers changes to a Discord Poll vote only.

Apr 2, 2025 - Create a Liquidity Pool for Retail DAO $RETAIL Token - DAO Treasury (Aragon): Link​

Proposal for creating a Liquidity Pool (LP) for the Retail DAO $RETAIL token and requests 150 million $RETAIL for that purpose from the Treasury. It also proposes setting up a Liquidity Bootstrapping Pool (LBP) to crowdfund the liquidity in ETH needed to fund the LP.

Jan 26, 2025 - Create Bounty Wallet + Budget for Hiring Members for Specific Tasks - Snapshot: Link​

  • Create a dedicated channel in Earn Tokens Category where bounty rewards are posted for specific tasks that are mostly marketing, social media and content creation related.
  • A dedicated, separate multisig wallet will manage the funds with a 2 signers requirement for transactions
  • A budget of 1m $RETAIL will be made available from the Main Multisig Wallet out of the unallocated budget.

Jan 18, 2025 - Reimbursement of Kevin & Establishing Support Team Multisig Wallet + Budget - Snapshot: Link​

  1. Reimburse Kevin: Cover tokens Kevin gave to members post-Genesis airdrop to prevent active members from being cut off by tokengate activation. Amount: $200,000 $RETAIL.
  2. Support Team Multisig Wallet: Establish a multisig wallet to address Season 1 Airdrop 1 issues at support team’s discretion (requires 2 signers for transactions). Budget: $150,000 $RETAIL.
  3. Fast track Access: "Airdrop" up to 600,000 $RETAIL tokens to onboard high-skill analysts or KOLs, granting tokens for immediate membership verification. Up to 30 new members.

Total Allocation: Use leftover tokens from Airdrop 1 in Main Multisig for a total of 950,000 $RETAIL tokens.

Dec 10, 2024 - Changing the Token Spending Thresholds for Proposals - Snapshot: Link​

Propose new token thresholds for spending proposal types to better align with the token ecosystem and streamline governance process. Proposed Thresholds:

  • Less than 20,000 tokens: poll only
  • 20,000-1,000,000 tokens: Snapshot only
  • 1,000,000 or more tokens: poll + Aragon

Main TGE proposal as published on Snapshot before $RETAIL minting process you can find it here​
