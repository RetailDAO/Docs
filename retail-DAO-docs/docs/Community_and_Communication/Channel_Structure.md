# Discord Channels Configuration: :gear:

This is the entry point for new visitors or participants and these channels are public (not token-gated).

- **Start Here** - read information how to join and how to get started (public)
- **Collabland-join** - verify wallet balance to claim membership role and get accesss to exclusive token-gated channels (public)
- **Support-contact** - submit a support ticket if you have questions or need help (public)

## Information

- **Announcements** (Public)
- **Leaderboard** - ranking of our most active members (Token-gated)
- **FAQ & Rules** - read our Frequently Asked Questions and the rules (Public)
- **Official Links** - in order to find our spaces easily and verify if links belong to us (Public)

## Earn Tokens 
Channels with token earning programs or specific token compensated roles

- **Hiring** - tasks, roles and jobs for Retail DAO and/or members that are usually compensated in $RETAIL are posted here (token gated).

## Coordination Zone (default: token gated)
- **Missions Center** - here is where you can join active DAO coordinated missions (each mission usually lasts up to 10 days).
- **Research Squads** - ongoing collaboration between members researching and diving deep on specific topics.
- **Coordinators Chat** - this is for Pro and VIP Members to coordinate efforts related to Missions and Research Squads.

## Insights Zone (default: token gated)

- **General Chat** - this is where the whole community comes together to stay on top of the news and developments as they occur.
- **Alpha Forum** - Thoughtful forum posts, sharing knowledge, insights and opportunities.
- **Trading Charts** - token gated, view only for basic tier members; Pro Members and higher can post here
- **VIP Chat** - token gated for Tier 3 VIP Members
- **Highlights** - showcasing token gated content from the DAO (public)

## Governance Zone (default: token gated)
- **Ideas for Improvement** - all members can suggest ideas to improve the DAO
- **Gov Forum** - Here is where concept governance proposals come to live, getting feedback and are being discussed until they mature to full, official proposals 
- **Proposals Voting** - All governance proposals are posted here, so they can be easily tracked (Aragon, Snapshot, Discord)

## Teams Zone (token gated, invite only)
These channels are for specific teams and members dedicated to these areas of expertise and where more in-depth discussions and collaboration take place on these topics. These teams channels are invite-only and while theoretically all members could be invited to join, preference generally goes to members with Pro Membership status or higher.
- **Partnerships Team**
- **Operations Team**
- **Product Team**
- **Marketing Team**
- **Treasury Team** 

## Admin Zone (token gated, private channels, invite only)
This Zone is for admin/moderation purposes and has a few extra operational channels too, including a channel for Contributors and a Moderators channel.


- ## Voice Channels (token gated)
- **General** - this is where weekly conference calls take place (Contributors calls and Coordination calls (AMA's))
- **Stage** - live broadcasting events where DAO members can jointly follow and react to events for example (i.e. FED meetings, congressional hearings etc.)
