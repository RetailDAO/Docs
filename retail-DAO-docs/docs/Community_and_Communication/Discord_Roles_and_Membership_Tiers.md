# CollabLand verification and membership tiers + Discord Roles

:::info

## Per June 9, 2025 these membership tiers with their associated roles will apply as follows:

:::

- Tier 1, Entry level (basic) - Role = **@VerifiedMembers** - requires a balance of _20,000 tokens_

- Tier 2, Advanced tier - Role = **@ProMembers** - requires a balance of _100,000 tokens_

- Tier 3, Premium Tier - Role = **@VIPMembers** - requires a balance of _250,000 tokens_


## What Is the Difference Between the Membership Tiers?
Every Membership tier builds on the next one and expands benefits, privileges, access and abilities. As RetailDAO continues to grow, we’ll keep adding exclusive perks and benefits to the various membership tiers ensuring our members always stay ahead of the curve! 

### Verified Members (basic tier)
Access to and participate in (most) channels in: 
- Coordination Zone (Missions Center + Research Squads)
- Insights Zone (General Chat, Alpha Forum and view-only access for Charts-Trading)

### Pro Members (advanced tier)
- Get all Verified Members benefits
- Ability to create new Research Squads, new Forum Posts and posting in the Trade-Charts channel.
- Voting weight of 3 in Mission Polls (deciding which next Missions the DAO should undertake)
- Several discount deals for crypto services

### V.I.P. Members (premium tier)
- Get all Pro Members benefits
- Access to the exclusive VIP Only channel where the crème de la crème of RetailDAO resides
- Free CryptoEQ Premium Membership included (max 50 seats availabe; only a few seats left!)
- Even higher voting power to decide new missions

## Can I Just Sell My $RETAIL Tokens After Receiving the Membership?
Members can sell their tokens anytime they wish, no strings attached. However, selling will automatically remove access to the exclusive membership channels within 24 hours.
