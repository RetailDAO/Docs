# RetailDAO Overview & Mission Statement

## Vision
Retail DAO is the definitive retail crowdsourced coordination layer on Base, where serious traders and professionals pool intelligence, act early with conviction, and systematically win together. 

## Our Mission :scroll:

We empower retail investors to stop missing opportunities by filtering signal from noise, surfacing high quality plays before they're mainstream, and providing the credible network you need to invest with conviction, backed by receipts, track records, and real skin in the game.

## Who We Serve

Retail crypto investors, traders, and working professionals who want to collaborate with other retail crypto investors on research & execution, take action on opportunities, and stop getting burned by anonymous gurus with hidden agendas.

## The Problem We Solve

**The Villain**: A relentless flood of noise, misinformation, and self-serving KOL’s that keeps retail investors systematically late to every meaningful opportunity.

**What's Broken**:
- Crypto moves at light speed and retail can’t keep up
- By the time alpha hits mainstream channels, the optimal entry is gone
- No accountability exists for anonymous calls and pump and dump schemes

**How It Feels**: You're constantly wondering if you’re going to miss your shot again this cycle, second guessing every move, watching opportunities run without you while drowning in conflicting signals.

**Why It Matters**: If a retail investor gives it their best, they should have a fair shot at success in the most open market in history.

## Our Solution

**No Gurus. Just Peers.** A stake-weighted collaborative intelligence network where influence correlates with $RETAIL holdings and documented results.

**We Keep Receipts**:
- Public DAO's Highlights with a selection of best insights and verified outcomes of our coordinated efforts.

**Aligned Incentives:** Token gated access ensures everyone has skin in the game. Higher stakes = higher influence on community decisions along with more abilities and responsibilities.
Quality Over Quantity: We don't compete on alpha,  we compete on reducing the noise and gaining a sustained edge through coordination.
