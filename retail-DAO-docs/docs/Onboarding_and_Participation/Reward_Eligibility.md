## For our current "2 for 1" Airdrop Campaign, (May-June 2025) for our 2nd Airdrop distribution here are the Guidelines:

### Airdrop Instructions

To participate in Retail DAO’s 2-for-1 airdrop, complete **all tasks** outlined below. Incomplete applications will **not** be included, so ensure all four tasks are successfully completed. 

:::info
Eligible participants will receive an airdrop of **60,000 $RETAIL**, with a maximum of **200 whitelisted recipients**.
:::

This airdrop is open only to participants who have not participated in previous Retail DAO airdrops. A separate a airdrop exists for current DAO members.

### Important Instructions

- **Complete All Tasks**: All four tasks must be completed to be eligible.
- **Verify Tasks**: Click "Verify" for each task to confirm completion.
- **Check Completion Status**: You’ve successfully completed the whitelisting process if:
  - All tasks have a green check mark.
  - The X.com thread task shows a white timer icon.
- **No Need to Claim**: You do **not** need to click "Claim Reward." The airdrop will be distributed automatically to your wallet approximately one week after the QuestN campaign ends.
- **Eligibility Limits**: Only 200 participants will be whitelisted. No additional submissions will be accepted once the whitelist is full.
- **No AI-Generated Content**: AI-generated content will be filtered out and is ineligible.
- **No New/Suspicious Accounts**: Brand-new or suspicious X accounts will be filtered out to prevent farming.
- **QuestN Support**: The Retail DAO team cannot assist with technical issues on the QuestN platform.

## Requirements

1. **Write a Thread or Article**:
   - Create a solid thread on X (at least three posts) or an article analyzing any Pre-TGE project listed on the [Kaito Pre-TGE Leaderboard](https://yaps.kaito.ai/).
   - Example of expected quality: [Sample X Thread](https://x.com/joincoinsider/status/1927403416617205956).
   - This may also qualify you for bounties or airdrops offered by the chosen Pre-TGE project (hence "2-for-1"), but check the project’s specific airdrop eligibility requirements, as Retail DAO cannot guarantee eligibility for other projects’ airdrops.
2. **Tag Retail DAO**:
   - At the end of your thread or article, include a sentence like: *This analysis was done in part for **@retaildao’s** 2-for-1 airdrop campaign.*
   - Ensure **@retaildao** is properly tagged in the post.
3. **Connect X Profile**:
   - Ensure your X profile is connected in QuestN and matches the profile used to post the thread. Submissions from different profiles will be rejected.
4. **Submit for Review**:
   - Submit your thread via the [QuestN](https://app.questn.com/quest/1039532022249431383) platform.
   - Submissions are manually reviewed for quality and substance at the reviewers’ discretion.
