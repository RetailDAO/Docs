# Problem that we aim to solve :handshake:

## The Problem

Retail investors face a relentless flood of information — new projects, competing narratives, and self-serving influencers all fighting for attention. The result is noise overload, where genuine insights are buried and meaningful opportunities are often recognized only after it’s too late. Retail investors are left chasing trends, second-guessing decisions, and struggling to keep up with faster, better-organized players.

## The Coordination Edge

RetailDAO turns scattered effort into organized intelligence. Through structured collaboration in targeted Missions, topic focussed Research Squads, and coordinated curation of knowledge sharing, our community collectively filters the noise to uncover what truly matters. Members contribute research, share data, and validate insights in real time — building a living network of credible, actionable knowledge.

## The Outcome

Instead of isolated individuals fighting information chaos, RetailDAO aligns collective focus around the best opportunities. This **Coordination Layer** gives retail investors a sustained edge in the market — helping them move earlier, act smarter, and succeed together in markets that never stop moving.
