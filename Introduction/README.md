# Introduction :speech_balloon:
**Retail DAO** was born from the belief that every retail investor deserves access to the same **knowledge, resources, and opportunities** as Wall Street insiders. We're building a home for like-minded individuals who are tired of navigating the investment landscape alone. 

By harnessing the power of collective wisdom and decentralized decision-making, we're democratizing access to the best research and alpha. We believe that by actively participating and contributing to the DAO, every member can access the exclusive Alpha Zone where valuable insights and opportunities await.
